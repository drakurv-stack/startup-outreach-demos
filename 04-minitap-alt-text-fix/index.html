Your site's images are invisible to screen readers

On https://www.minitap.ai/, 125 of 144 <img> tags ship with an empty alt="" attribute (counted in the raw HTML). That includes customer logos, testimonial avatars and feature screenshots — every one of them is silently skipped by assistive tech, and invisible to image search. The fix, as a checklist

  • Customer logos: alt="Acme Mobile logo" — name the company, drop the word "image".
  • Testimonial avatars: alt="Priya Nair, QA lead" — person + role; the quote text stays in the markup, not the alt.
  • Feature screenshots: one-sentence summary of what the screenshot shows.
  • Decorative icons/dividers: keep alt="" — intentionally empty so screen readers skip them (this is the one correct use).
  • Add a lint rule (e.g. eslint jsx-a11y/alt-text) so no new image ships without an alt decision.
