# Demo 03 — 1club pricing-page clarity (concept)

**Target:** 1club — https://1club.ai (Sofia, Bulgaria; AI-native gym management software; seed 2.2M EUR Oct 2026)

## The issue (verified on the live site, 2026-10-12)

On https://1club.ai/pricing (confirmed in raw HTML):

1. The "Grow Your Brand Online" card lists **"Gym Website 49/month. No setup fees (included in Grow)"** — no `$` sign.
2. The pricing FAQ on the **same page** answers "What if I only want a website?" with **"We offer a standalone Gym Website for $99/month."**

Same product, two different prices on one page ($49 vs $99), plus the add-on prices ("49/month", "99/month") are missing the `$` sign used everywhere else ($63 / $239 / $369).

## The fix (this demo)

A redesigned "Grow Your Brand Online" section: two clearly labeled options (bundled $49/mo vs standalone $99/mo) with honest feature differences, consistent `$` formatting everywhere, and a corrected FAQ entry. Mobile-first, single self-contained `index.html`.

> The dollar amounts come from the live page; the founder picks the real numbers. The demo proposes the *disambiguation*, not the prices.

> Concept demo only — not affiliated with 1club. Built to show the fix, not to impersonate.
